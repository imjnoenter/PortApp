# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individual investors managing their own stock portfolio. They check the app multiple times daily during market hours, and occasionally outside hours for planning and review. The job: track positions, plan entries/exits, monitor allocation, and review performance — all in one place without switching between tools.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio tracker that consolidates holdings, live quotes, transaction history, plans, and analytics into a single-file web app. No backend infrastructure — powered by Google Sheets as the data layer and hosted on GitHub Pages. Success looks like: the user opens one tab and has everything they need to make informed trading decisions.

## Brand Personality

Bold, Modern, Data-driven — the app carries a warm earthy identity (terracotta, sage, honey) that distinguishes it from generic fintech. It feels confident and personal, not institutional.

## Anti-references

- **Generic fintech** (navy gradients, gold accents, stock-photo heroes) — this is a personal tool, not a bank's marketing site.
- **Dense terminals** (Bloomberg, old-school trading platforms) — data should breathe; no overwhelming grids of tiny numbers without hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. **Information density with breathing room** — show a lot of data, but use hierarchy, spacing, and progressive disclosure so nothing feels cramped.
2. **Glanceable first, explorable second** — stat cards and color-coded alerts surface what matters; detail lives in modals and expandable sections.
3. **One source of truth** — every number traces back to the sheet; no stale caches, no conflicting displays.
4. **Personal, not institutional** — warm tones, profile pictures, and per-user configuration make it feel like YOUR tool.
5. **Zero friction** — no build step, no login flow, no onboarding wizard. Open the tab and go.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. 4.5:1 minimum contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text. Reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`. The app serves both light and dark themes with warm and cool tone variants.
